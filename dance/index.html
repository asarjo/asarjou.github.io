How to play

  1. Set up your camera. Put your laptop or webcam at about chest height and step back 2–3 m so your arms fit in the frame.
  2. Mirror the coach. The coach faces you, so move like you're looking in a mirror. If their arm goes up on the left of the screen, raise the arm on your left.
  3. Read the move cards. Cards slide in at the bottom right. Hit the pose on the card when it reaches the glowing frame. Arrows show how to move into it.
  4. Nail the gold moves. Gold cards are worth a big bonus.
  5. Collect stars. Every move is rated PERFECT, GREAT, GOOD, OK or MISS. Fill the bar to earn up to five stars, then go for DIAMOND and LEGEND.

No camera? Pick Free dance to follow along without scoring, or Autoplay to watch a demo.
